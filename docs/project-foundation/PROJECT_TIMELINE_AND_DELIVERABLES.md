# CITS5206 Master Timeline: Chronological Graded Deliverables & Execution Protocol

This document outlines the strict, chronological sequence of all graded components and critical project milestones for CITS5206.

⚠️ **CRITICAL GRADING PREMISE:** The final grade is inherently weighted towards individual accountability. **Individual Component = 70%** (25% Test + 45% Individual Project/Professionalism); **Group Component = 30%** (D1 + D3).

---

## 🔄 Continuous Graded Components (Weeks 1 – 12)

*These items form the evidence base for your "45% Individual Project Assessment" and "Professionalism" grades. Failure to maintain these will result in severe proportional mark deductions.*

* 👤 **Fortnightly: Individual Professional Reflective Journal**
  * **Submission:** Via LMS.
  * **Requirement:** A structured reflection on soft skills, ethical considerations, and team dynamics, drawing upon the *Beyond Coding* textbook and Thursday workshops.
* 👥 **Weekly: Team Accountability Documents**
  * **Submission:** Saved in the designated MS Teams channel by the end of each week.
  * **Requirement:** Authored by the rotating Project Manager (PM). Must explicitly detail what *each* member achieved that week.
* 💻 **Continuous: GitHub Commit Logs**
  * **Submission:** Project Code Repository.
  * **Requirement:** Continuous, micro-level commits with your distinct user ID. This is the ultimate forensic evidence used by the Facilitator to validate your D2 Individual Technical Grade.

---

## 📅 Chronological Milestone Execution

### Week 1: Project Allocation & Foundation

* **Action:** Submit project bidding preferences via LMS by Thursday 23:59.
* **Protocol:** Once allocated to the "Cultural Heritage Fire Risk System," immediately establish the MS Teams channel, create the GitHub repository, and dispatch the introductory email to the Client (Sven) and your Group Facilitator.

### Week 2 – 4: Requirements Gathering & D1 Preparation

* **Action:** Conduct the initial Client meeting.
* **Protocol:** Nominate the first Project Manager (ensure you hold this role at least once during the semester). Formally request and document Client approval for the use of AI-generated code (Tier 2 requirement).
* **D1 Pre-work:** Begin defining the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) boundaries and assigning specific individual technical modules for the upcoming D2.

### Week 5: Deliverable 1 (D1)

* 👥 **Assessment:** **Project Spec and Plans Group Submission**
  * **Weight:** **20%** (Core of the Group Mark).
  * **Format:** One PDF submission per group via LMS. Must contain live URLs to GitHub and MS Teams.
  * **Content:** Problem statement, Client communication proof (MVP agreement), project schedule, risk/tech assessment, and *explicit allocation of individual tasks for D2*.
  * ⚠️ **Constraint:** **NO 48-hour late penalty waiver applies to group work.** 5% deduction per day late.

### Week 6: Peer Evaluation 1

* 👤 **Assessment:** **Group Member Evaluation 1 (Feedback Fruits)**
  * **Weight:** **Grade Moderator** (Directly impacts your 20% D1 mark).
  * **Format:** LMS via Feedback Fruits.
  * **Protocol:** Must be completed within exactly one week of the D1 submission. If peers report you as a "free-rider," your D1 group mark will be unilaterally downgraded by the Facilitator.

### Week 7: The Theoretical Examination

* 👤 **Assessment:** **In-class Written Test**
  * **Weight:** **25%** (Individual Essay Component).
  * **Format:** Invigilated, in-person written examination.
  * **Content:** IT ethics, social impacts, and software project management methodologies.
  * ⚠️ **Constraint:** **Tier 1 Assessment.** Strictly closed-book. ZERO AI assistance permitted.

### Week 8 – 9: Intensive Individual Development

* **Action:** Execution of your assigned technical module (e.g., the risk algorithm, the map rendering, or the database architecture).
* **Protocol:** **Strict Code Citation.** Any code snippet sourced externally or generated via AI (ChatGPT/Copilot) MUST be cited in the comments strictly adhering to the *MIT Guide to Citing Code Sources*. Uncited AI code constitutes Academic Misconduct.

### Week 10: Deliverable 2 (D2)

* 👤 **Assessment:** **Individual Project Assessment**
  * **Weight:** **45%** (The most critical individual grade; includes Professionalism/Journals).
  * **Format:** Individual submission via LMS.
  * **Content:** Delivery of your specific technical components. You must provide exact hyperlink references to your personal GitHub commits/branches to prove individual authorship.
  * **Requirement:** Must include a formal declaration of how AI was utilized during development (Tier 2 Assessment rule).

### Week 11: System Integration & Client UAT

* **Action:** Merge individual code branches. Conduct User Acceptance Testing (UAT) with the Client.
* **Protocol:** Capture written feedback/approval from the Client validating that the MVP has been successfully delivered.

### Week 12: Deliverable 3 (D3)

* 👥 **Assessment:** **Final Group Delivery**
  * **Weight:** **10%** (The remainder of the Group Mark).
  * **Format:** One submission per group via LMS.
  * **Content:** Final functional software repository handover, final system documentation, and proof of Client acceptance tests.
  * ⚠️ **Constraint:** **NO 48-hour late penalty waiver.**

### Week 13 (Exam Period): Peer Evaluation 2

* 👤 **Assessment:** **Group Member Evaluation 2 (Feedback Fruits)**
  * **Weight:** **Grade Moderator** (Impacts D3 and final Professionalism adjustments).
  * **Protocol:** Evaluates final integration efforts and verifies that every team member successfully rotated through at least two distinct roles (e.g., PM, Coder, Tester, Documenter) with documented evidence.
